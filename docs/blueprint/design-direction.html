Thesis

Three materials, one discipline

BAUBC sits where agriculture, technology and business strategy meet. The system turns that meeting point into three materials. Each one has a job, and lime is never used for decoration.

Field paper

Calm surfaces for reading and work

Applications, tasks, ledgers and articles live on warm off-white paper with ink-dark text.

Canopy

Deep green for cinematic moments

Heroes, DECODED episodes and the Summit stage use the canopy band. It is also the portal's dark mode.

Signal

Bio-lime for what is live or verified

At most one lime element per screen: the primary action, a live seat count, or a verified stamp.

Colour

Palette and the contrast rules behind it

Greens carry the brand. Harvest amber is the warm complementary accent for awards, DECODED and warnings. Soil red and river blue appear only as status colours.

Typography

Bangla and English as equals

Every family is open source (SIL OFL) and self-hosted in production. Bangla gets its own families, taller line-heights and no letter-spacing tricks. Conjuncts are checked at every heading weight.
